01 · Where cleaning businesses leak money

The cleaning is usually fine. Everything around it is where jobs get lost.

A great clean is not enough when the owner is also the receptionist, the scheduler, the recruiter, the trainer and the sales team. Something always slips. This playbook fixes that.

Slow replies

Leads go cold

A quote request sits for a day and the client books whoever answered first.

No pipeline

Feast or famine

Busy month, quiet month. Nobody is calling new accounts when the calendar is full.

Mixed standards

Every cleaner does it their way

One missed skirting board turns into a complaint, then a lost regular.

Hiring stress

New cleaners learn by guessing

No clear first week means slow starts and people quitting early.

03 · Cold calling system

A steady flow of new accounts, even when you are busy cleaning

WDT calls new accounts every week, busy or not. The goal of every call is simple: book a free walkthrough quote. Nothing more.

List A

Offices and clinics

Small offices, dental and medical clinics, gyms. Recurring weekly contracts.

List B

Property managers and agents

End of lease, move-in, move-out cleans. They need someone reliable on short notice.

List C

Short-stay hosts

Airbnb and holiday rentals. Turnover cleans with photo reports they can trust.

08 · Onboarding cleaners

From job ad to trusted cleaner in two weeks

I handle the ad, the screening and the paperwork. You only meet the people worth meeting.

Step 1

Post the job ad

Short, clear ad on local job boards and Facebook groups. Three screening questions built into the application so the wrong fits filter themselves out.

Step 2

10-minute phone screen

I check availability, transport, experience and attitude. I send you a shortlist with my notes.

Step 3

Paid trial clean

They join a senior cleaner on a real job. The senior cleaner scores them on the job sheet. You decide yes or no.

Step 4

Paperwork and setup

Contract, ID, any checks required in your area, pay details, group chat invite, uniform and kit list. All tracked so nothing is missing.

Step 5

First week plan

Day 1 shadow. Days 2 and 3 clean with a buddy. Days 4 and 5 solo with photo review. End of week check-in call with me.

Step 6

30-day review

Client feedback, photo scores, punctuality. Pass means they join the regular roster.
